Altitude, weather, medical

The house sits at about 9,600 ft. Take the first afternoon easy, drink water all day, go light on alcohol the first night, and run the humidifiers. Each family with a baby under 2 should mention the altitude at their next pediatrician visit.

Altitude with kids

  • Kids get altitude sickness too, but Children's Hospital Colorado calls it something to be aware of, not to fear. A kid with a cold or ear infection is more likely to have symptoms
  • Babies and toddlers show it as unexplained fussiness, poor sleep, poor feeding, vomiting or unusual tiredness. Altitude sickness does not cause fever
  • If a child is unwell at altitude, assume altitude until proven otherwise and get care or go lower
  • Diamox isn't usually prescribed for healthy kids; a pulse oximeter isn't recommended unless your doctor gives you one
  • The manager offers 10% off oxygen rental if anyone needs it

Weather, early to mid January

  • Highs in town low 20s to about 30°F, lows near 0°F. Mornings below zero are common; wind chill on lifts is colder
  • About 10 inches of snow in town over 17 snow days; far more on the upper mountain
  • Daylight roughly 7:20 am to 4:50 pm
  • UV is intense at altitude even when it's cold. Sunscreen every morning
