This does not shrink the lodging. Last year's list was 7 couples plus Mom and the nanny. Dropping Josh and Alyssa takes the room count from 8 to 7, but the headcount goes up because of the babies (19 confirmed vs the 16 I searched against last week). That matters in Vail specifically: the Town of Vail caps short-term rental occupancy, and both single houses on the list (184 W Meadow Drive and the 7BR Gore Creek house) are capped at 16. Whether infants count is up to each host, so both need a message before you rely on them. The two-condo Northwoods setup sleeps 26 and has no such problem.

The other shift is what the kids need. Five children aged 4 and under means cribs, pack-and-plays and a room with a monitor, not a bunk room. A bedroom next to the parents, or a two-bed room for Mom and the nanny with the 4 year old, is worth more than a six-bunk room.

Trip length: 7, 8 or 9 days

Travel days book-end the trip, so a 9 day trip is 8 nights and 7 ski days. All three land on Erin's birthday (Sunday January 10) with the group on the mountain.

9 days: Thu Jan 7 to Fri Jan 15
8 nights, 7 ski days. Recommended. Both weekend days are mid-trip. Depart Friday morning ahead of MLK weekend traffic. Every option below is available on these dates.
8 days: Fri Jan 8 to Fri Jan 15
7 nights, 6 ski days. Arrive Friday, which puts you on I-70 with the Denver weekend crowd (build in an extra hour). Weekly discounts kick in at 7 nights on the Gore Creek house.
7 days: Fri Jan 8 to Thu Jan 14
6 nights, 5 ski days. Cheapest lodging total but the worst per-night value: no weekly discounts, and the Gore Creek house actually costs more for 6 nights than for 7.

Alternative 7 night option: Thu Jan 7 to Thu Jan 14 avoids the Friday arrival. I priced Jan 8 to 15 for consistency; nightly rates are flat across these midweek January dates so the totals will be within a few hundred dollars.

Recommendation

Book Northwoods, both 6-bedroom units, for 8 nights (Thu Jan 7 to Fri Jan 15): $62,894, about $8,985 per confirmed group. Listings: 6BR Chalet (Vrbo), Juniper 311 (Vrbo), Northwoods at Vail Realty. It is the only Vail option that is true ski-in/out, has enough real bedrooms for the confirmed list plus Josh and Alyssa plus two spare couples, sits next to the Golden Peak children's ski school and nursery, and has no occupancy-cap problem with five small kids. If Josh and Alyssa drop and you do not fill the spot, you still want the second unit: it is where Mom, the nanny and the kids sleep, and it keeps a quiet unit for nap and bedtime shifts while the party continues in the Chalet.

Every confirmed adult group has a door and a king or two real beds; three rooms stay open for the maybes and any replacement couples. The Chalet's 14 person dining table and terrace are the evening room. Cribs and pack-and-plays are not listed as provided at Northwoods; Vail Realty or a rental service (Baby's Away Vail) can supply them, and I would ask when booking.

If you want to spend less

The 7BR Gore Creek house at $19,726 for 7 nights is a third of the price and has exactly 7 rooms for 7 confirmed groups (king, five queens, one queen plus twin, plus a two-bed bunk room). It requires the host to accept 19 heads over a stated cap of 16, it is a 10 minute walk or a bus to the lifts, and it is dated. Worth one message to the host if the group would rather bank the savings for dinners and a chef night.

If you want to spend more

184 W Meadow Drive is the showpiece, but it is capped at 16 and has 6 real couple rooms, so with 19 to 22 heads it only works if the host allows infants and one couple takes the two-double room. The Four Seasons pairing at $177k is the no-compromise luxury answer for the confirmed list only.

All Vail options in detail

Full write-ups with amenities, location notes, pros and cons and scores for each of these are in the September 4 version of this page (version history). What changed for the new group:

  • Northwoods Chalet + Juniper 311: moves from a good fit to the clear pick. Nine adult-capable rooms plus two bunk rooms, sleeps 26, both 6BR units verified available for all three trip lengths. Links: Chalet, Juniper 311, Vail Realty direct.
  • 184 W Meadow Drive: drops from 2nd to 3rd. Cap of 16 and 6 couple rooms were a compromise for 16 people and are a real problem for 19 to 22. Vrbo, Airbnb.
  • Arrabelle: now needs four apartments instead of three to clear 19 heads, pushing it to about $75,800 for 8 nights with still no shared living room. Drops to 5th. Booking.com.
  • Manor Vail: holds 4th. Three condos sleep 24, across the street from the kids' ski school, four hot tubs, breakfast included, but bed types are still unverified and there is no group room. Booking.com, 800-950-8245.
  • 7BR Gore Creek House: stays the value pick and now has exactly the right room count, but the 16 cap is a hard question for the host. Vrbo.
  • Northwoods Chalet alone: new entry as the budget fallback if the group ends at 7 rooms and the host will treat the four under-2s as infants. Sleeps 14 on paper.

Next steps

  • Decide 8 nights vs 7. The Northwoods pair is $9,272 more for the 8th night and 7th ski day, about $1,325 per group.
  • Message Vail Realty (or book through Vrbo) for both Northwoods units on Jan 7 to 15. Ask: is Vail's 10.8% lodging tax inside the Vrbo total, can they hold both units together, and can they arrange cribs and pack-and-plays. Vrbo terms are 50% within 24 hours, non-refundable inside 60 days, so lock the guest list before Nov 8.
  • Give Josh and Alyssa a soft deadline. With 12 bedrooms you can hold their room until the November payment without changing the booking.
  • Book Golden Peak children's ski school and the Small World nursery as soon as 2026-27 booking opens; January weeks fill early and the 4 year old and both 2 year olds qualify for different programs.
  • Buy Epic passes before prices rise again and before sales close in early December.
